The book

No Money No Problem

How to build a real estate empire when you have nothing to start with.

They didn’t give you the roadmap. This is it.

Most real estate books are written by people who got lucky in a hot market, hired a ghostwriter, and packaged it as a system. No Money No Problem is not that book.

I was a high school dropout and a union ironworker with no industry connections, no family money and no business degree. Nobody handed me a path into real estate. I built one from scratch in one of the most expensive, competitive markets in the country. Today I run an active brokerage, manage a portfolio built on off-market and development deals, and coach agents through the same frameworks I developed by doing the work.

What this book is

A practical guide to acquiring real estate without relying on conventional financing, a strong credit profile, or a pile of cash sitting in savings. It is written specifically for the people the industry ignores: the ones who want in but do not fit the traditional mold.

You will learn:

  • How to identify and structure deals without using your own money or credit
  • The frameworks I used to go from zero to an active portfolio in a high-cost market
  • How to stop trading time for commissions and start building assets that work without you
  • Why most agents stay broke, and the specific decisions that separate investors from order-takers
  • How to design a real estate business around your life, not the other way around

What this book is not

This is not a motivation book. I do not do affirmations, vision boards, or vague advice about mindset. If you are looking for someone to tell you that you can do it, this is not your book. If you want to know exactly what to do next, it is.

Who it is for

  • Active agents who want to stop depending entirely on commissions
  • First-time investors who feel priced out of their own market
  • Anyone who grew up without financial advantages and is tired of being told real estate wealth is out of reach

Your circumstances don’t define your ceiling. Your decisions do.
